# CCPlayTV

## Short description

A player for your own Xtream/M3U playlists, with live TV, movies and series organized by category.

## Long description

CCPlayTV plays and organizes Xtream/M3U playlists you already have. The app does not include any channel, movie or series: all content comes from the list you add, provided by third parties, and its availability depends on your provider.

- Add an Xtream list (server, username and password) or an M3U URL; each list becomes a profile on the start screen.
- Live TV, Movies and Series with the categories your list declares, a program guide when the list provides one, search and channel switching with the remote control.
- Favorites, history and resume where you left off, stored on the TV itself.
- Hide categories and lock them with a PIN (parental control).
- Radio: public stations from the Radio Browser directory.
- Posters and synopses from TMDB when you enter your own TMDB key.
- Interface in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Lists, credentials and the catalog are stored on the TV. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
